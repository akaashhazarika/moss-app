Moss Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Moss is made by Xapla Ventures ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Moss uses and what happens to it. In short: Moss has no account, no servers and no ads. Your screen time, blocked apps and progress stay on your phone. Moss uses Meta's app events tool only to measure whether our ads on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) lead to installs and purchases.

Information Moss uses, on your device only

  • Screen time: with your permission (Usage access), Moss reads how long each app was in the foreground, to show your stats, score your daily goal and enforce time limits.
  • Which app is open: with your permission (the "Moss app blocker" Accessibility service), Moss checks the name of the app that comes to the foreground, so it can pause apps you chose to block. It does not read the content of your screen, what you type, or anything inside other apps, and it does not keep a history of the apps you open.
  • Your settings and progress: your goal, chosen apps, schedules, time limits, focus sessions, points, streaks and plants.
  • Your installed apps: the names and icons of launchable apps, so you can choose which ones to block.

All of this is stored in Moss's private storage on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, including Meta. Device backups of Moss data are turned off.

Purchases

If you buy Moss Pro, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We don't receive your payment details. Google shares limited purchase information with us (for example, an order ID and purchase status) so Moss can unlock Pro. Google's handling of your information is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Ad measurement (Meta)

Moss includes Meta's App Events SDK so we can tell whether our Meta ads work. It sends Meta: that Moss was installed and opened, that you finished setup, and that you started a trial or bought Moss Pro (with the plan and price), along with device information such as your device's advertising ID, model and OS version, and your IP address. It does not send your screen time, the apps you use or block, or anything else listed above. Meta's handling of this information is covered by Meta's privacy policy.

You can limit this by deleting or resetting your advertising ID (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android phones).

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We don't show ads in Moss. We don't build profiles about you.

Children

Moss is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can revoke Usage access or turn off the Moss app blocker at any time in your device settings. To delete everything Moss has stored, clear Moss's storage in your device settings or uninstall Moss.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA or similar laws), you may have rights to access or delete personal information. Because Moss keeps your screen time, settings and progress only on your device, you can view and delete them yourself as described above. Ad measurement data is held by Meta; you can make requests about it through Meta's privacy settings. If you have questions, contact us.

Changes

If we change how Moss handles information, we'll update this policy and the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is material.

Contact

You can reach us through the developer contact details on Moss's Google Play listing.
